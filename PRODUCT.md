# Earnit

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose
Earnit is an iPhone app that lets people earn time for selected distracting apps through effort. This repository is its static marketing website, in English and Spanish.

## Users
People who want to make scrolling intentional and put daily movement before distracting apps. Audience inferred from existing landing content.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The landing explains the walking workflow: read steps from Apple Health, convert them into minutes, spend them on selected apps, and restore app restrictions when the session ends. Apple Screen Time provides restrictions. iOS 18 or later, per the verified App Store listing (2026-10-24). The 1,000 steps / 5 minutes rate is an illustrative example inherited from the project, not a universal fixed rate.
The current listing also describes study and push-up rewards; these are outside this landing redesign's walking-led narrative. Exact pricing must come from the App Store rather than the old landing's outdated annual price.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve Earnit, the forest-dark and cobalt identity, and the English/Spanish language switch. The user explicitly requested Vaultaire's patterns: immediately visible phone, clear app proposition, and direct App Store acquisition. Remove the cinematic background video. User approved an illustrative app interface on 2026-10-24.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing AppHome.dc.html and assets/earnit-appstore.png provide interface references. Existing scenic assets may support the in-phone illustration. Existing landing testimonials and rating are explicitly marked placeholders and must not be presented as evidence.
Verified Apple listing: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6804905841 ; seller Joaquin Balthasar De Weert; website https://www.earnitscreen.com .

## Product Principles
- Make it immediately clear this is an iPhone app.
- Demonstrate the steps-to-screen-time mechanism in the product interface.
- Preserve working legal, support, language, and download paths.
- Use verified product facts; never invent ratings, customers, or testimonials.
