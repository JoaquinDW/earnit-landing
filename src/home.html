Still have a question?
We’re here to help.

How does Earnit count my steps?

With your permission, Earnit reads your step count from Apple Health. Your iPhone already records your steps, so you don’t need a separate tracker or wearable.

Which apps does it block?

You pick the apps you want to manage using Apple’s Screen Time controls. Earnit applies restrictions to your selection, so you stay in control of what’s included.

What happens when my time runs out?

Your selected apps are shielded again when your session ends. Earn more minutes through movement before starting your next session.

Is 1,000 steps always worth 5 minutes?

That’s the example shown here. You can set a step-to-minute rate in the app that works for your routine.

Does my health data leave my phone?

Your health and motion data stay on your iPhone. Apple’s Screen Time framework also keeps your selected apps private from us. See our privacy policy for more information.

Which phones are supported, and what does it cost?

Earnit is available for iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Download it from the App Store, where you can see current in-app purchases and subscription options before choosing a plan.
