Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

How does Earnit block apps?

It uses Apple's Screen Time framework. The apps you choose stay shielded until you start a session with minutes you earned. When the session ends, the shield comes back automatically.

What counts as effort?

Three things today: steps from Apple Health, focused study sessions, and pushups counted by your iPhone camera.

How many minutes do I earn?

You set the rate. A common starting point is 1,000 steps for 5 minutes, and you can make it stricter or gentler whenever you like.

Can I just skip the block?

There is no snooze and no one more minute button. Earnit adds honest friction rather than a cage, so the way through is always a walk, a study session or a few reps.

Is the pushup camera recording me?

No. Reps are counted on device and the video is never saved or uploaded.

What does it cost?

App blocking and time earning are part of an Earnit subscription. You'll see the current price in the App Store, and you can cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Which devices does it support?

Earnit is built for iPhone.
