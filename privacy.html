Privacy

Your data never leaves your phone.

Earnit has no account system and no server that stores anything about you. That is not a policy choice we could quietly reverse. It is how the app is built.

What Earnit reads

Your daily step count, from Apple Health, with your explicit permission. Earnit reads that number and nothing else from your health record. It never writes to Apple Health.

What Earnit sees about your apps

Apple's Screen Time framework lets Earnit lock and unlock the apps you choose without telling us which apps those are. The selection is held by iOS and stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There is no sign up, no email address and no advertising identifier. If you buy the paid tier, Apple handles the payment and shares no personal detail with us beyond the fact of the purchase.

Crash reports

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymised crash logs. You can turn that off in Settings, Privacy and Security, Analytics and Improvements.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything Earnit stored goes with it, because all of it lived on the device.

Getting in touch

Questions about any of this go to support@earnit.app.
