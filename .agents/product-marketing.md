# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-26

## Product Overview
Earnit is an iPhone app for earning access to selected distracting apps through walking, study and supported exercises. Steps come from Apple Health; earned minutes are spent in deliberate sessions, after which restrictions return. Requires iOS 18 or later. Version 1.3.2 was checked against the App Store on October 26, 2026. Release history also documents squats.

## Audience and Jobs
People managing their own distracting app use, including students and people who want movement before scrolling. Audience inferred from the product and existing website, not customer interviews. Jobs: put effort before access; make breaks deliberate; connect movement or study with a limited app session.

## Differentiation and Alternatives
Earnit connects an earned-minute balance with selected-app restrictions. Comparison dimensions: effort before access, scheduled blocking, opening interventions, task rewards, activity reporting and focus-session motivation. Relevant alternatives include Opal, Freedom, one sec, ScreenZen, Forest, Unrot, PushBlock and PushUp Time. Each has distinct use cases; do not claim universal superiority.

## Voice and Evidence
Direct, practical and nonjudgmental. Use concrete mechanisms and clear limitations. No fabricated ratings, testimonials, performance tests or promised reductions. Do not describe the product as a treatment. Health/camera permissions and app selection must be described precisely; do not make a blanket claim that no analytics exist.
