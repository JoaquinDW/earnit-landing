How we chose

We compared the commitment each option asks you to make: finish effort before access, reserve a period, cover your devices, or observe a daily boundary. The goal is to create focus blocks with a purpose, not to reproduce Forest’s visual design.

Earnit publishes this guide and is included in the comparison. Picks are editorial recommendations by use case, based on official product information checked on October 26, 2026; they are not a hands-on performance ranking.

1. Earnit — A usable reward after effort

Earnit converts walking, focused study and on-device push-up counting into minutes for selected apps.

Consider this if you like the idea of a reward but want it connected to a later app session. Define the work first and the break second. It is a different motivation model: the reward becomes something you choose to spend after earning it.

Official product details: Earnit

2. Opal — Repeating the same focus window

Opal schedules app and website blocks, with configurable unblock rules.

This direction suits a routine that has a reliable start time. Put the boundary around a real task and keep required tools available. A repeating rule can reduce setup decisions, but you still need to decide what the protected time is for.

Official product details: Opal

3. Freedom — Treating several screens as one workspace

Freedom synchronizes app and website blocking sessions across supported devices.

Consider this when focus fails because the next distraction is on another device. Evaluate your workspace as a whole before switching. A session that looks successful on the phone may still miss your goal if the same feed is open on the computer.

Official product details: Freedom

4. ScreenZen — A straightforward daily rule

ScreenZen offers schedules and daily limits through a donation-supported app.

Start here if your goal is a practical boundary rather than a focus metaphor. Choose a small number of restrictions you can explain clearly. The useful comparison is how the rule fits a normal day, including necessary app access, rather than how elaborate the dashboard looks.

Official product details: ScreenZen

Which one should you choose?

Name what you would miss from Forest before leaving: the timer, the visual record or the start-of-session ritual. Then choose the alternative that improves the part that is failing. You may not need to change if the original commitment already fits.

Check current compatibility, available features and purchase terms before choosing. Earnit requires iOS 18 or later; current plans and trial eligibility are shown on the App Store.

A useful question before you install

Is Earnit the same kind of reward as a virtual tree?

The relevant distinction is what you earn. A virtual focus record represents the session; earned app minutes can be spent on later access. Choose the consequence that makes sense for your own routine.
