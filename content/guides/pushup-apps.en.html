How we chose

We selected products whose official information explicitly connects exercise counting with app access. We then compared how easily you can explain the rule, whether the routine works where you use your phone, and how you would spend the reward.

Earnit publishes this guide and is included in the comparison. Picks are editorial recommendations by use case, based on official product information checked on October 26, 2026; they are not a hands-on performance ranking.

1. Earnit — Push-ups alongside walking and study

Earnit converts walking, focused study and on-device push-up counting into minutes for selected apps.

This is the fit when push-ups are one way to earn rather than your only rule. Think about the next session before doing the set: earning minutes is useful when you want to decide how much time to spend, then return to the restriction.

Official product details: Earnit

2. PushBlock — A direct exercise condition

PushBlock uses camera-counted push-ups as the condition for unlocking selected apps.

A focused mechanism may appeal if you already know the movement you want before social media. Before committing, check the current unlock behavior and purchase terms. Favor a rule you can explain in one sentence over an elaborate setup you will keep changing.

Official product details: PushBlock

3. PushUp Time — Exercise-based access windows

PushUp Time counts exercise reps on-device and offers timed app access.

Consider this when you want the reward to have a defined window. Work backward from the break you want: would its available access duration fit that break? A movement requirement is only useful if the time it opens matches the boundary you intended.

Official product details: PushUp Time

Which one should you choose?

Try the camera setup in the room where you expect to use it. Check framing, counting feedback and the resulting unlock before building a routine around it. If exercise is not appropriate for the moment, choose a study or scheduled-blocking approach. Read how walking earns minutes for another option.

Check current compatibility, available features and purchase terms before choosing. Earnit requires iOS 18 or later; current plans and trial eligibility are shown on the App Store.

A useful question before you install

Is every workout automatically converted into screen time?

Do not assume that. Compare the activities each app explicitly supports and how it verifies them. An app that counts push-ups does not automatically promise to recognize every exercise or import every workout.
