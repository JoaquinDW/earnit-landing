How we chose

We compared when you make the access decision. Some people want to reconsider an opening; others want a condition established before the urge appears. Neither preference makes one product universally better. The useful question is which rule you will keep.

Earnit publishes this guide and is included in the comparison. Picks are editorial recommendations by use case, based on official product information checked on October 26, 2026; they are not a hands-on performance ranking.

1. Earnit — Making access depend on effort

Earnit converts walking, focused study and on-device push-up counting into minutes for selected apps.

This is the option to consider when a pause alone is not the rule you want. Decide on an activity before the moment of temptation. The value of the switch is that you can describe what needs to happen before the next session begins.

Official product details: Earnit

2. ScreenZen — Managing a daily allowance

ScreenZen offers schedules and daily limits through a donation-supported app.

A daily ceiling may fit if you want to keep casual access within a boundary. Decide what counts as necessary use before choosing the allowance. Judge the experience over several normal days rather than making the first limit unusually strict.

Official product details: ScreenZen

3. Opal — Deciding the time window in advance

Opal schedules app and website blocks, with configurable unblock rules.

Consider this for periods that should stay free of selected distractions, regardless of whether you feel tempted. A predictable work or study window is a clearer test than a vague promise to use the phone less throughout the entire day.

Official product details: Opal

4. Unrot — Finishing a task before access

Unrot exchanges completed tasks for points that unlock selected apps.

This approach can fit if the main problem is postponing something concrete. Define an observable finish line for the task. A task-based boundary becomes easier to evaluate when you can say what was completed before access, rather than just whether you waited.

Official product details: Unrot

Which one should you choose?

Pick the decision you want to move: the opening, the start of a work period or the completion of a task. Try one rule with one app, then review whether the new decision point feels practical. Read our Instagram comparison for daily limits versus earned access.

Check current compatibility, available features and purchase terms before choosing. Earnit requires iOS 18 or later; current plans and trial eligibility are shown on the App Store.

A useful question before you install

Does switching require giving up all social media?

The shortlist is about managing selected access. Decide what you want to keep, what should happen first, and when a session should end. A specific rule is easier to assess than an all-or-nothing promise.
