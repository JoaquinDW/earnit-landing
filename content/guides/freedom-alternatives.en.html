How we chose

We narrowed this comparison to personal iPhone routines. The deciding question is whether you need coordinated device coverage or simply a better rule on the phone you keep reaching for. Keep Freedom on the shortlist when synchronized sessions are the main requirement.

Earnit publishes this guide and is included in the comparison. Picks are editorial recommendations by use case, based on official product information checked on October 26, 2026; they are not a hands-on performance ranking. Our comparison methodology.

1. Earnit — An earned break from work

Earnit converts walking, focused study and on-device push-up counting into minutes for selected apps.

Choose this direction when you want the break itself to depend on something completed first. Decide whether movement or study is a realistic prerequisite. It is a different contract with your phone, rather than a reason to expect desktop-wide control.

When to consider another option: If your main requirement is coordinated blocking on a computer and phone, compare Freedom first. For an iPhone routine, check iOS 18 compatibility and whether earning access fits better than a fixed allowance.

Official product details: Earnit

2. Opal — Predictable phone blocking periods

Opal schedules app and website blocks, with configurable unblock rules.

Consider this if your working hours are stable and the phone is the main source of interruption. Map the schedule to a real work block and allow necessary tools. Evaluate it as a deliberate phone routine rather than comparing every possible platform feature.

When to consider another option: If your difficulty is deciding whether to open an app in the moment, compare an opening intervention. If you want to complete an activity before access, compare an earning rule. Choose a schedule when protecting a time window is the main job.

Official product details: Opal

3. ScreenZen — A simpler purchase decision

ScreenZen offers schedules and daily limits through a donation-supported app.

Start here when trying another subscription is the obstacle to getting a boundary in place. Keep your first rule small. A workable limit on the app that interrupts you most is a better experiment than rebuilding your entire digital environment on day one.

When to consider another option: Choose a limit when you want a defined allowance. If your goal is to finish an activity before a break, evaluate that condition separately; a quantity of available time and a prerequisite answer different questions.

Official product details: ScreenZen

4. Forest — Committing to one focus session

Forest pairs focus sessions with virtual trees, app blocking and focus history.

This can fit a work sprint whose beginning and end need to feel concrete. Define the task before starting, then check the result afterward. Choose it for the session experience; if unified device blocking is essential, compare that requirement separately before switching.

When to consider another option: If the question is how much time you actually spend in each app, compare Apple Screen Time’s activity report. If you want a completed study session to earn later app access, compare that reward model with a visual focus record.

Official product details: Forest

Which one should you choose?

List the devices you actually use during work. If distraction jumps between them, do not discard coordinated blocking casually. If it stays on your phone, try one of these options on a single work block and judge the interruption you wanted to prevent.

Check current compatibility, available features and purchase terms before choosing. Earnit requires iOS 18 or later; current plans and trial eligibility are shown on the App Store.

A useful question before you install

Should I replace Freedom if it already covers my devices?

A switch makes sense when the new rule addresses a specific unmet need. If synchronized sessions are working for you, a phone-focused alternative may add setup without solving a new problem.
