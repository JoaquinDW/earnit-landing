How we chose

This shortlist compares three measurement jobs rather than declaring three identical products. A useful tracker should help you make a decision: which app to limit, whether your earning rule fits, or whether you are returning to focused work consistently.

Earnit publishes this guide and is included in the comparison. Picks are editorial recommendations by use case, based on official product information checked on October 26, 2026; they are not a hands-on performance ranking.

1. Apple Screen Time — Understanding actual app usage

Apple Screen Time reports app activity and lets you set usage limits.

Start here if you cannot yet name the app or period you want to change. Review a day and a week before setting a target. Separate useful phone activity from the scrolling you wanted to reduce, or a lower total could become the wrong goal.

Official product details: Apple Screen Time

2. Earnit — Reviewing an earned-access routine

Earnit converts walking, focused study and on-device push-up counting into minutes for selected apps.

This fits the question “What effort did I put before my access?” rather than simply “How many minutes was my phone active?” Review the rule as a cycle: earning, choosing a session, and returning to the boundary. Compare it with the usage report when evaluating the result.

Official product details: Earnit

3. Forest — Seeing time dedicated to focus

Forest pairs focus sessions with virtual trees, app blocking and focus history.

Choose this perspective if your goal is to show up for a planned task. A completed focus session and a low phone-usage total are not the same measure. Check the output of the session as well as its length so the record supports the work you care about.

Official product details: Forest

Which one should you choose?

Pick one question for the week. For usage, review the same app and device consistently. For an earning routine, check whether the exchange is practical. For focus, record a concrete output alongside session time. Our Instagram guide turns a usage observation into a specific limit.

Check current compatibility, available features and purchase terms before choosing. Earnit requires iOS 18 or later; current plans and trial eligibility are shown on the App Store.

A useful question before you install

Does less total screen time always mean better progress?

Choose the behavior you want to change first. Reading course materials and checking a feed may both appear as phone use, but they have different purposes. Judge your rule against the activity it was meant to change.
