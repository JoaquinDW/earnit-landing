How we chose

We compared alternatives by the reason someone might leave a scheduled blocker. The shortlist covers four different decisions instead of pretending every product does the same job. Opal’s own schedule documentation is the reference for the starting point.

Earnit publishes this guide and is included in the comparison. Picks are editorial recommendations by use case, based on official product information checked on October 26, 2026; they are not a hands-on performance ranking.

1. Earnit — Effort before access

Earnit converts walking, focused study and on-device push-up counting into minutes for selected apps.

This is a meaningful switch if you want a condition rather than another timetable. Decide whether a walk, a study session or push-ups would be a reasonable prerequisite for your next scroll. Earnit is a stronger fit for that question than for coordinating a whole office’s devices.

Official product details: Earnit

2. ScreenZen — A donation-supported alternative

ScreenZen offers schedules and daily limits through a donation-supported app.

Start here if the payment model is your main reason for looking beyond Opal. Compare the rules you actually need before adding more complexity. A daily boundary that you understand can be more useful than collecting features you never configure.

Official product details: ScreenZen

3. Freedom — Coordinating phone and computer

Freedom synchronizes app and website blocking sessions across supported devices.

Put this near the top if a blocked phone simply sends you to a laptop. The relevant test is whether your actual devices can join the same blocking routine. For a phone-only habit, a broader device setup may be more than you need.

Official product details: Freedom

4. one sec — Interrupting the opening habit

one sec offers app-opening interventions and scheduled blocking.

Choose this when your difficult moment is the reflex to open an app, even before a long session starts. Compare the interruption experience with a fixed block. The right choice depends on whether you want to reconsider the opening or make access conditional on earlier effort.

Official product details: one sec

Which one should you choose?

Write down your reason for switching before installing anything: cost, opening habit, earned access or multiple devices. Test one alternative against that reason with one distracting app. For a practical boundary example, see our Instagram limit guide.

Check current compatibility, available features and purchase terms before choosing. Earnit requires iOS 18 or later; current plans and trial eligibility are shown on the App Store.

A useful question before you install

Which alternative is closest to Opal?

For schedules and daily boundaries, start by comparing ScreenZen. If your reason for switching is a different relationship with access itself, consider Earnit. Similarity is useful only when you want to keep the original rule.
