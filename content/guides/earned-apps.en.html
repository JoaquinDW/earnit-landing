How we chose

We compared the earning action, the meaning of the reward, and how well each rule fits an ordinary day. A screen time reward should be understandable before you install: minutes, points and a timed unlock are different commitments.

Earnit publishes this guide and is included in the comparison. Picks are editorial recommendations by use case, based on official product information checked on October 26, 2026; they are not a hands-on performance ranking.

1. Earnit — A flexible earned-minute balance

Earnit converts walking, focused study and on-device push-up counting into minutes for selected apps.

Choose this if your days vary. A walk might fit one afternoon and a study session the next. Earnit is especially relevant when you want the reward to be a short, deliberate session rather than permission to scroll for the rest of the day.

Official product details: Earnit

2. Unrot — A task-based reward system

Unrot exchanges completed tasks for points that unlock selected apps.

Consider this when the activity you want to finish matters more than a particular movement. Decide which tasks deserve access before starting. A broad task menu can suit a varied routine, but it also makes a clear personal rule more important.

Official product details: Unrot

3. PushUp Time — Exercise before an unlock window

PushUp Time counts exercise reps on-device and offers timed app access.

A timed access window can be easier to reason about than a growing balance. Check the current exercise choices and unlock durations in the listing. Pick this format when you want each exercise session to have a clear, immediate connection to access.

Official product details: PushUp Time

4. PushBlock — A push-up-first boundary

PushBlock uses camera-counted push-ups as the condition for unlocking selected apps.

Consider a narrow rule if a large settings menu would make you postpone setup. The decision is concrete: do you want push-ups to stand between you and selected apps? Choose another mechanism for situations where that movement is impractical.

Official product details: PushBlock

Which one should you choose?

Before subscribing, restrict one app and complete one earning cycle. Check that the reward feels proportionate, that access ends when expected, and that essential tools remain available. Our walking guide explains the balance model in more detail.

Check current compatibility, available features and purchase terms before choosing. Earnit requires iOS 18 or later; current plans and trial eligibility are shown on the App Store.

A useful question before you install

Do these apps pay you money?

Here, earning means access to selected apps. It does not mean cash, cryptocurrency or App Store credit. Compare the access rule itself rather than treating the word “reward” as a financial offer.
