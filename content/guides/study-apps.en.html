How we chose

We considered the start of a study session, the boundary during it, and the transition into a break. A good study lock should leave learning tools available and make the next step clear when the session ends.

Earnit publishes this guide and is included in the comparison. Picks are editorial recommendations by use case, based on official product information checked on October 26, 2026; they are not a hands-on performance ranking.

1. Earnit — Earning the next break

Earnit converts walking, focused study and on-device push-up counting into minutes for selected apps.

Consider this if completing the work before social media is the rule you want. Define a concrete study task before starting. Earning time can connect the task with the break, but the timer alone cannot tell whether you understood what you studied.

Official product details: Earnit

2. Forest — Making a session visible

Forest pairs focus sessions with virtual trees, app blocking and focus history.

This is a natural fit when showing up for a session is the hard part. Give the session a task, not just a duration. A visible record can be a useful prompt to return tomorrow, while your actual learning still needs its own check.

Official product details: Forest

3. Opal — A recurring study timetable

Opal schedules app and website blocks, with configurable unblock rules.

Consider schedules when your study periods repeat. Work backward from your classes and essential learning apps rather than blocking everything. This fits a predictable timetable better than a rule that must be earned anew for every short break.

Official product details: Opal

4. Freedom — Studying on several devices

Freedom synchronizes app and website blocking sessions across supported devices.

If your notes are on a laptop and your distractions move between screens, evaluate the setup as one study environment. Keep required reference sites available. A phone-only solution is unlikely to address the entire problem when the next tempting tab is on another device.

Official product details: Freedom

Which one should you choose?

Before the next session, write one output you want: a solved problem, a revised page or a short recall exercise. Choose the app rule around that output and plan a definite stopping point for the break. A walk between sessions is another way to create a transition.

Check current compatibility, available features and purchase terms before choosing. Earnit requires iOS 18 or later; current plans and trial eligibility are shown on the App Store.

A useful question before you install

Is a timer enough to block distracting apps?

A countdown and an access restriction are separate capabilities. Check whether the product applies the boundary you want, and keep the apps needed for your course outside the distraction list.
