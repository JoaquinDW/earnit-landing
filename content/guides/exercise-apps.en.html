How we chose

We compared the activity input, the access reward and the practical setting. A routine that works at home may be awkward during travel or a work break. The best fit is the option whose supported action matches your day without making the rule ambiguous.

Earnit publishes this guide and is included in the comparison. Picks are editorial recommendations by use case, based on official product information checked on October 26, 2026; they are not a hands-on performance ranking.

1. Earnit — Mixing walking with earned sessions

Earnit converts walking, focused study and on-device push-up counting into minutes for selected apps.

Consider this when you want movement to accumulate toward a later break. Check the available earning methods in the current app, including the documented squat option in its release history. Choose a practical exchange and a session length before treating the system as a routine.

Official product details: Earnit

2. PushUp Time — A defined window after exercise

PushUp Time counts exercise reps on-device and offers timed app access.

Consider the window model when the end of access matters as much as the movement before it. Compare its current supported exercises with the activities you actually want to do. An impressive exercise menu is less useful than one repeatable action that fits your surroundings.

Official product details: PushUp Time

3. PushBlock — Keeping the exercise rule narrow

PushBlock uses camera-counted push-ups as the condition for unlocking selected apps.

A single clear prerequisite can fit someone who already wants a push-up routine before selected apps. Check setup where you plan to use it. If you need walking or another activity instead, prioritize documented support for that activity over the appeal of a simple rule.

Official product details: PushBlock

4. Unrot — Treating activity as a broader task

Unrot exchanges completed tasks for points that unlock selected apps.

Consider the task-reward direction when your desired prerequisite is broader than a counted movement. Verify how the current app handles the task you choose; do not assume that listing a workout means automatic rep verification. The distinction matters if objective counting is your main requirement.

Official product details: Unrot

Which one should you choose?

Start with the activity and context, then compare the reward. Check the permissions, run one complete cycle and confirm when restrictions return. The walking walkthrough explains steps-to-minutes, while the Instagram guide covers a daily cap as another approach.

Check current compatibility, available features and purchase terms before choosing. Earnit requires iOS 18 or later; current plans and trial eligibility are shown on the App Store.

A useful question before you install

Which app supports every type of workout?

This comparison does not establish that any option verifies every workout. Read the current supported-activity list. Choose based on the movement you intend to do and whether you want counted activity or a broader task rule.
