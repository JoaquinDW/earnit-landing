How we compare apps

Clear product facts. Recommendations for a particular need. A visible connection to the publisher.

Who publishes these guides?

Earnit publishes this website and its guides. Earnit is an iPhone app that turns walking, focused study and supported exercises into minutes for selected apps. Its App Store listing names Joaquin Balthasar De Weert as the developer. See the official listing.

Earnit appears in our comparisons because it is relevant to earned screen time. We have a commercial interest in people choosing it. Our guides are publisher comparisons, rather than independent product reviews.

How we choose and compare

  1. Define the reader’s decision: earned access, scheduled blocking, focus sessions, opening interventions or activity tracking.
  2. Check product mechanisms against official listings, help pages and policies. Link to those sources beside the relevant product and at the end.
  3. Explain who an option fits and when another approach is worth considering. A numbered list organizes the choices; it is not a measured performance ranking.
  4. Keep facts separate from editorial judgment. A recommendation is our assessment of the documented mechanism, not evidence of a guaranteed result.

We do not present these comparisons as hands-on benchmarks. Any future practical test should describe its setup, date and observations and provide evidence. We do not invent test results, ratings, user counts or testimonials.

What the information does and does not establish

Official pages establish what a publisher documents. They do not establish that an app performs identically on every device or that it will reduce anyone’s usage by a particular amount. Current compatibility, features, prices and trials should be checked before purchase.

Applyra helps us research App Store search terms. Its traffic and difficulty scores are not Google search volumes or predictions of placement in an AI answer. We group related terms when one comparison can answer the same decision.

Dates, assistance and corrections

Each comparison shows the date its official sources were checked. Publication and modification dates describe the article; a routine site rebuild does not renew its source-check date. We may use AI for drafting and translation, and check the claims against the linked sources.

If you spot an outdated feature or an unclear comparison, contact Earnit support with the page and the product detail that needs correction. We update the article when its facts or recommendations change.
